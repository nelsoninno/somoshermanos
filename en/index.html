Our belief

Born from one firm belief

Somos Hermanos began more than five years ago from a simple idea: that small actions, repeated every Sunday, can change the course of a child's life. We believe the world gets better when we treat each other as brothers.

“Whoever loves God must also love their brother.”

1 John 4:21

None of this would be possible without the help of God, and without every person who chooses to share something good with those who need it most.

What we do

Sunday after Sunday, baskets that reach those who need them

We bring food help baskets to children in the Sonsonate area. Each basket arrives with food and, very often, with a handwritten letter for a child.

Frequently asked questions

What you usually ask

What is Somos Hermanos?

Somos Hermanos is a Salvadoran foundation, the social arm of Tacos Hermanos. Since 2021 it has delivered help baskets to children who need them in the Sonsonate area, every Sunday of the year.

How can I donate a basket?

Buy a help basket (or as many as you like) at any Tacos Hermanos restaurant. You can write a message to the children, and that letter travels with your basket.

When is my basket delivered?

Your basket is delivered the same Sunday. If your donation is made after Thursday, it is delivered the following Sunday.

Where are the baskets delivered?

In the Sonsonate area of El Salvador. We work hand in hand with the Sunday Bible School of Iglesia Bitinia to reach the children.

Can I write to the children?

Yes. When you buy a basket you can leave a handwritten message. Each letter arrives with the basket, and many children keep it as their favorite part.

What is the relationship with Tacos Hermanos?

Somos Hermanos was born from Tacos Hermanos and is its social arm. Tacos Hermanos donates more than 300 baskets every Sunday, and the community adds their own from the restaurant.
